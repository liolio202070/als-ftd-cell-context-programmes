# Data availability

## Manuscript-ready draft

All datasets analysed in this study are publicly available from their original repositories or accompanying source publications. The principal public accessions are GSE219279, GSE219280, GSE153960, GSE212630, GSE163122, GSE296710 and GSE296714; the ALS GWAS summary statistics are available under GCST90027164. The Gittings C9orf72 single-nucleus assets and the Ruf same-nucleus RNA-ATAC resource are identified through their source articles and the dataset manifest distributed with this archive. Panel-level Source Data for all main figures and Extended Data Figures 13–14, compact supplementary tables, data dictionaries and file-level checksums are available at **https://github.com/liolio202070/als-ftd-cell-context-programmes**; the corresponding immutable Zenodo DOI will be added when the archive record is finalized. Large third-party raw matrices are not redistributed and should be retrieved from the original repositories under their applicable terms.
